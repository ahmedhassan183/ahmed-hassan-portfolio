## Scope and standard of proof

Sources reviewed: the current two-page resume (`public/Ahmed-Hassan-Sales-Business-Development-Resume.pdf`); `data/sales.ts`, `data/experience.ts`, and English/Arabic portfolio copy; all six supplied work previews in `public/work/`; `public/work/README.md`; and the earlier Phase 3 project brief supplied by Ahmed. The project contains no underlying CRM workbook, contact log, sent-quotation register, original Solar PV workbook, signed maintenance agreement, 90-day completion log, sales report, or Kahla performance report. The previews are sanitized presentation/excerpt images, not independently auditable source files.

“Verified” below means supported by an available source at the stated level. It does **not** mean independently confirmed against company records. HIGH = directly visible in an artifact or unambiguous role documentation; MEDIUM = Ahmed's resume/brief and/or a derived preview without the original; LOW = insufficient evidence. A publishable statement must retain the exact scope and must avoid confidential customer or company data. “YES WITH CONTEXT” means the fact may be stated only with its limitation (for example, *mapped market entries*, not customers acquired). No estimates, plans, targets, or demo outputs are treated as achieved sales results.

## Claims excluded from publication as results

- **Innovation commercial outcomes:** any number of contacted prospects, responses, meetings, qualified opportunities, quotations sent, negotiations, closed deals, acquired customers, revenue, pipeline value, sold kW/MW, repeat business, referrals, partner accounts, maintenance subscriptions or renewals.
- **Kahla performance:** any sales count/value, monthly target or attainment, ranking, customer count, upsell rate, branch growth, or retention rate. “Closed retail sales” is supported only qualitatively.
- **Plans and forecasts:** two-year roadmap, 90-day targets, scheduled meetings, proposed KPIs, projected revenue or contract counts as if achieved.
- **Artifact figures as business outcomes:** 47 market entries are not customers; 20 queued accounts are not contacts; 13 sprints are not completed sprints; 3 tiers are not sold agreements; 40 sheets/1,500+ formulas are not sales impact; demo PV capacity/energy/quotation are not customer installations or sold projects.
- **Role and professional scope:** no head/director-of-sales title, team-size/management claim, or electrical-engineering licensure without separate evidence. Keep Innovation's official title **Growth Manager**.
- **Private material:** customer identities, contact details, pricing, margins, internal company data or unredacted records. Audit evidence privately before any public use.
